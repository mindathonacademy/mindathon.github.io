Questions parents ask

Common questions, answered

Is Mindathon suitable for my child?
Mindathon is designed as a learning and engagement game for students and families. For guidance on the most suitable version for your child's age and needs, please contact the Mindathon team.
Can parents play with their children?
Absolutely. Mindathon is designed as a group activity — it's just as enjoyable when parents play alongside their children.
Do we need any special equipment?
No screens or apps are needed. Mindathon is a complete physical game set. Please contact the team for details about what's included.
