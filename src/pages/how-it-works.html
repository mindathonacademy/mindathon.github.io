Keep it simple

No rules overload. Just play.

Mindathon is designed to be understood quickly. A host explains each challenge, everyone plays a round, and the next challenge begins.

  • Easy to start — learn each challenge in seconds.
  • Variety keeps it fresh — different challenges, different skills.
  • Works in groups — family, classroom or training centre.
