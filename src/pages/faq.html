Home/FAQ
What is Mindathon?
Mindathon is a physical cognitive training game. Through hands-on challenges, players practise skills like memory, focus, problem solving, mental speed and flexibility.
Who is Mindathon for?
Mindathon is designed for students, families, schools, training centres and educational organisations. Please contact the Mindathon team for guidance on the most suitable version for your needs.
Is Mindathon a board game?
Mindathon is a physical game, but it is not a traditional board game — there is no board. It is built around cognitive challenges that players respond to with speed and accuracy. The best way to understand it is to try it.
What skills does Mindathon focus on?
Mindathon is designed to practise six cognitive areas: memory & recall, focus & attention, problem solving, mental speed & accuracy, mental flexibility, and confidence & positive attitude. See the Cognitive Skills page for details.
Is Mindathon suitable for students?
Yes. Mindathon was created as a learning and engagement game for students. For guidance on age suitability and programme options, please contact the Mindathon team.
Can parents play with their children?
Yes. Mindathon is designed as a group activity and is a great choice for family time — parents and children can play together.
Can schools conduct Mindathon programmes?
Yes. Mindathon can be run as workshops, school programmes or training sessions. Visit the For Schools page, or contact us to discuss your institution's needs.
How can I purchase Mindathon?
To enquire about purchasing Mindathon, visit the Get Mindathon page or message us on WhatsApp at 017-766 4345.
How can I bring Mindathon to my school?
Contact the team via the Contact page or WhatsApp, and we'll discuss workshops, programmes and partnership options for your school.
Where is Mindathon available?
Please contact the Mindathon team for current availability and programme details.
