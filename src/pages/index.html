The Game

A game you can actually hold

Mindathon is a physical cognitive skills training game. Players take part in challenges that call on their memory, focus, speed and problem-solving — responding with their hands, voice and full attention.

Instead of watching or tapping a screen, players are actively thinking, responding and participating. That makes brain training feel like a game, not a chore.

  • Physical game — no app required, no account required.
  • Hands-on challenges — you take part, you don't just watch.
  • Play solo or in groups — family, classroom or friends.
  • Designed for repeated practice — play again and again.
