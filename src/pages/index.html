What is Mindathon?

A physical game that turns brain training into play

Mindathon is a cognitive skills enhancement training game. Players take part in challenges that call on their memory, focus, speed and problem-solving — responding with their hands, voice and full attention.

Instead of watching or tapping a screen, players are actively thinking, responding and participating. That makes brain training feel like a game, not a chore.

  • Hands-on challenges — you take part, you don't just watch.
  • Made for groups — play with family, classmates or friends.
  • Designed by a memory trainer with 33 years of experience.
Why Brain Training?

Skills that benefit from practice

Just as physical activity exercises the body, regular mental challenges give cognitive skills opportunities to be practised and developed.

Practise, not just watch

Active responding builds engagement in a way passive watching cannot match.

Structured challenge

Clear challenges at rising difficulty give learners a steady, meaningful path.

Fun keeps people coming back

When training feels like a game, people willingly practise again and again.
