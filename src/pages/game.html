The product

Meet the Mindathon game

A real, physical game — designed, built and played hands-on.

What Mindathon is

Challenges. Thinking. Responding.

Mindathon presents cognitive challenges that players must think through and respond to — using their memory, focus, speed and reasoning.

Because it is a physical game, everyone plays together in the same room. No phones, no screens, no autoplay — just active participation.

  • Think first, respond fast — challenges reward attention and accuracy.
  • Play together — built for families, classrooms and groups.
  • Made by an experienced memory trainer — 33 years of training expertise.
The experience

What playing Mindathon feels like

Fully present

Every round demands attention — there is no background mode.

Playfully fast

Rounds move at a pace that keeps thinking sharp and energy high.

Social by nature

Players encourage, react and celebrate together as they play.

Rewarding

Getting a challenge right feels earned — effort turns into small wins.

Why hands-on?

Active participation changes the experience

Watching a video or tapping a screen keeps the mind mostly passive. Mindathon gets people actively thinking, responding and participating.

Passive learning

You watch, you listen, you absorb — and often, you drift.

Active participation

You think, you respond, you act. Every round engages the mind.
