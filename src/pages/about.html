What is Mindathon?

A physical cognitive training game

Mindathon is a physical game designed around cognitive skill development. Through hands-on activities and challenges, players practise skills such as memory, focus, problem solving, mental speed and flexibility.

It is a product of Mindathon Academy, founded by Master Alagan Govindan — a memory trainer with 33 years of experience — together with co-founder K. Kumaran, who brings 20 years of experience in marketing.

Why Mindathon was created

Training that people actually enjoy

Memory and cognitive training can change the way people learn — but only if people stay engaged. Mindathon was created to turn training into something people want to do, again and again.

Engagement first

Training works best when people look forward to it. Mindathon is built around play.

Hands-on by design

Active responding keeps players involved in a way that screen time cannot.

Built for togetherness

Families, classrooms and groups can play together — learning side by side.

Our approach

Practice through participation

Mindathon's approach is simple: present a challenge, let players think, and have them respond actively. Repeating this cycle gives cognitive skills regular, meaningful practice.

  • Clear challenges — every round has a goal.
  • Active participation — thinking, responding, doing.
  • Progressive practice — challenges grow as players improve.
  • No screens — a physical, social, hands-on experience.
Our vision

Making brain training a normal part of growing up

We believe cognitive skill practice should be as natural as play itself — available to every family, student, school and community that wants it.

Our mission

Hands-on training, made engaging

Our mission is to provide meaningful, hands-on cognitive training experiences that support learning and personal growth — through a game people genuinely enjoy playing.
